Chicken Feet

A Beloved Dim Sum Classic

In China, chicken feet are poetically called "phoenix claws" and have been enjoyed for centuries as a popular delicacy. They are a staple on dim sum menus, where they are traditionally deep-fried, steamed, and braised in a rich, savory black bean sauce until fall-off-the-bone tender.

Popular Street Food and Snacks

Beyond tea houses, chicken feet are one of the most popular packaged snacks and street foods across the country. Varieties like tangy pickled pepper chicken feet from Sichuan are sold everywhere from night markets to convenience stores, making them a favorite everyday treat.
